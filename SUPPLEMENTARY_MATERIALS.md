# Supplementary Materials

The model is intended to be read together with the article supplementary material. A public release should include the final supplementary PDF and workbooks used for journal submission:

```text
Supplementary_Material.pdf
Supplementary_Material_A_Cost_Model.xlsx
Supplementary_Material_B1_Suppliers_Processing_Wind_Farm_4COffshore.xlsx
Supplementary_Material_B2_Turbine_Supply_Chain_Sourcing_Inputs.xlsx
Supplementary_Material_C1_NACE2_Cost_Mapping.xlsx
Supplementary_Material_C2_Wages_Calculation.xlsx
Supplementary_Material_D_Detailed_Results.xlsx
```

The executable notebook uses the B1, B2, and C1 workbooks as inputs for sourcing and industrial classification. B1 and B2 are retained in the complete local publication archive but are excluded from GitHub until redistribution permission for their TGS 4C Offshore and WindEurope-derived content is verified. Raw TGS 4C Offshore files are proprietary and are not required when approved processed inputs are available.
